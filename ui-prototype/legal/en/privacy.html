Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-25

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mind Map web app (the "Service") handles information. The Service is designed so that the content you create stays on your device.

1. Maps and Images

Maps, edit history, and images you add are stored only in your browser's storage on your device. They are not sent to our servers. Deleting browser data deletes them.

2. Camera Images

When you turn on hand control, camera images are analyzed on your device to recognize hand shapes. Images and video are not recorded, stored, or sent to our servers.

3. Voice Input

Voice input uses your browser's speech recognition. Depending on your browser, audio may be sent to the browser vendor's speech service (for example, Google or Apple) and handled under that vendor's privacy policy. The Service does not store audio.

4. Access Logs

The Service is delivered through Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect against abuse, technical information such as IP address, browser type, and access time may be processed by Cloudflare. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Fonts

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when displaying them.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy when we deem it necessary. The revised Policy takes effect when posted on this page.
